# Product Sheet: CoolStack CS-40 Mini-Fridge (Oakridge Home Goods, fictional)

## Overview
The CS-40 is a 40-litre mini-fridge for bedrooms, offices and small kitchens.
Model number: CS-40. Weight: 9.2 kg.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 40 litres
- Power: 65 W, 220 to 240 V
- Energy use: 0.6 kWh per day
- Temperature range: 2 °C to 10 °C
- Noise level: 38 dB
- Maximum room temperature for use: 32 °C
- Colours: white or graphite
- Price: 189.00 EUR

## Doors and installation
The door hinge is reversible. A hinge-swap kit is included, and changing the hinge side takes about 20 minutes.
Not for outdoor use.

## Care
The CS-40 defrosts automatically, so no manual defrosting is needed.
Wipe the interior with a damp cloth. Unplug the unit before cleaning.

## Warranty
Parts are covered for 2 years. The compressor is covered for 5 years.

## Delivery
Orders ship within 3 to 5 business days.
