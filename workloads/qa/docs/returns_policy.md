# Returns and Refunds Policy (Oakridge Home Goods, fictional)

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
Holiday extension: items bought between 1 November and 31 December may be returned until 31 January.

## Condition
Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
Opened small appliances can be returned, but a restocking fee of 15% of the item price applies.
The restocking fee does not apply to defective items.

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 business days after our warehouse receives the item.
Shipping charges are non-refundable, except when the item was defective or sent in error.

## Defective and wrong items
Defective items qualify for free return shipping and a replacement within 5 business days.
If we sent the wrong item, we pay for return shipping and send the correct item at no charge.

## Excluded items
Clearance items are marked "FINAL SALE" and cannot be returned.
Gift returns receive store credit instead of a refund.
